Salads

Tofu Stir-Fry

Ingredients

1 can tuna, drained

1/4 cup mayonnaise

1 celery stalk, chopped

1/4 onion, chopped

Lettuce leaves for serving

Recipe

Mix tuna, mayonnaise, celery, and onion.

Spoon onto lettuce leaves.

Serve as a salad or wrap.

Ingredients

2 cups romaine lettuce, chopped

1 avocado, sliced

2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced

1/2 cup cooked bacon, chopped

1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled

1 tomato, diced

1/4 cup ranch dressing

Recipe

Arrange lettuce on a large plate.

Place rows of avocado, eggs, bacon, cheese, and tomato.

Drizzle with ranch dressing.

Serve chilled.
