Meat Meals

Beef Tacos

Ingredients

500g ground beef

Taco shells or tortillas

1 onion

1 tsp cumin

1 tsp paprika

Lettuce, tomato, cheese

Recipe

Cook onion and beef together.

Add spices and simmer 5 minutes.

Fill taco shells with beef and toppings.

Serve immediately.

Chili Con Carne

Ingredients

500g ground beef

Kidney beans

Tomatoes

Onion

Chili powder

Garlic

Recipe

Brown beef with onions and garlic.

Add tomatoes, beans, and spices.

Simmer 30 minutes.

Serve with rice or bread.
