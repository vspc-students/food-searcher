Cobb Salad

Ingredients

2 cups romaine lettuce, chopped

1 avocado, sliced

2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced

1/2 cup cooked bacon, chopped

1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled

1 tomato, diced

1/4 cup ranch dressing

Recipe

Arrange lettuce on a large plate.

Place rows of avocado, eggs, bacon, cheese, and tomato.

Drizzle with ranch dressing.

Serve chilled.

Chili Con Carne

Ingredients

500g ground beef

Kidney beans

Tomatoes

Onion

Chili powder

Garlic

Recipe

Brown beef with onions and garlic.

Add tomatoes, beans, and spices.

Simmer 30 minutes.

Serve with rice or bread.
